# Visual Bible - NestGen HeyGen + Imagine Art Example

FORMAT: 16:9, 720p for tests, 1080p for final hero shots.

CHARACTER: Original straw-hat pirate host, red open jacket, blue cropped trousers, yellow
waist sash, energetic but professional. Do not use copyrighted names, logos, franchise
symbols, or exact protected character designs.

WORLD: A cinematic pirate command ship upgraded into a physical-AI operations vessel:
weathered wood, brass instruments, blue holographic route maps, drone docks hidden in the
deck, and industrial islands representing mines, ports, solar farms, railways, utilities,
and public safety.

VISUAL STYLE: Premium cinematic anime-real hybrid, consistent background language, high-trust
enterprise documentary composition.

CAMERA: Slow push-ins, controlled dolly moves, aerial reveals, map transitions, and static
medium presenter shots for HeyGen.

COLOR: Sea blue, weathered red, brass gold, charcoal black, signal cyan UI, restrained amber
alerts.

PROMPT RULE: Every Imagine Art shot prompt is this bible plus the shot-specific
`visual_prompt` and `camera`. HeyGen presenter shots reuse the same character/world language
for background and motion direction.
